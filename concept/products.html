Cold storage panels

Cold storage.

Hygienic-finish panels engineered to hold a tight, consistent temperature for refrigerated and frozen storage, clean rooms and food-processing spaces.

PIR core · tongue-and-groove with gasket

PIR Insulated Metal Panel (IMP)

A 4 in (100 mm) rigid PIR foam core between 0.5 mm satin steel faces, R-30 (RSI 5.3), with a locking tongue-and-groove joint. 1,000 mm (39.37 in) cover width, lengths from 10 to 36 ft (3.0 to 11 m), in ten colors. Other thicknesses available: 2, 6 and 8 in.

PIR core · hygienic TPO or PVC finish

USP Coldbox TPO/PVC Panel

An insulated panel with a hygienic TPO or PVC finish, ideal for cold storage rooms, clean rooms, and processing areas needing moisture resistance and easy maintenance.

PIR/PUR core · monolayer vinyl finish

USP Box Vinyl Panel

A monolayer wall panel with a rigid PIR/PUR foam core and a durable vinyl finish suited for wash-down and sanitation-sensitive environments.

PIR/PUR core · monolayer vinyl finish

USP Wall Vinyl Panel

A monolayer wall panel companion to the Box Vinyl line, insulated with rigid PIR/PUR foam for consistent cold-storage wall performance.
