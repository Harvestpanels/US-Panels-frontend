02 · Exterior walls

Mineral wool
exterior wall panels

Insulated exterior wall systems built to protect critical equipment with a tight, secure, weather-resistant shell. Choose how the fasteners show.

At a glance

  • Self-supporting, with a tongue-and-groove joint
  • Insulated with mineral rockwool
  • Exposed fastening, or concealed fastening for a smooth, uninterrupted finish
Mineral wool core · through-fastened, saddle washer

USP Fireguard Wall Panel: Exposed Fastening

A self-supporting wall panel with a tongue-and-groove joint, insulated with mineral rockwool and secured with a visible through-fastened, saddle-washer system.

Mineral wool core · concealed fastening

USP Fireguard Wall Panel: Hidden Fastening

The same self-supporting mineral wool wall panel with a concealed fastening system on each side for a smooth, uninterrupted finish.

PIR core · tongue-and-groove with gasket

PIR Insulated Metal Panel (IMP)

A 4 in (100 mm) rigid PIR foam core between 0.5 mm satin steel faces, R-30 (RSI 5.3), with a locking tongue-and-groove joint, 1,000 mm cover width and ten colors. A foam-core option for walls and ceilings.
