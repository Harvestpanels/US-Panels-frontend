What is FM certification?

Factory Mutual (FM) Global is similar to Underwriters Laboratories (UL) in that both are focused on safety. FM Approvals is the independent testing division of FM Global, and it focuses on researching and testing products to ensure they meet only the highest standards for property loss prevention and safety. Only products that bear an FM Approved mark conform to these standards and have met the ongoing requirements of the FM certification process.

Panel foam

PIR core.
Built to perform.

Our panels use PIR (polyisocyanurate) foam, sometimes called "Class 1" foam, a mixture of polyol, isocyanate, and cyclopentane engineered specifically for insulated buildings.

01

High structural strength

A rigid, closed-cell core that adds real mechanical strength to the panel, not just insulation value.

02

High fire resistance

PIR chemistry is formulated to resist ignition and limit how quickly fire spreads across the core.

03

Low smoke emission

Produces meaningfully less smoke than standard PUR foam when exposed to fire.

04

Stable thermal performance

Maintains its insulating value under sustained heat exposure, instead of degrading early in a fire event.

PUR and mineral wool (rock wool) cores are also available for projects with different fire, cost, or performance requirements.

What one panel replaces

Insulated Metal Panels compress a typical wall or roof buildup into a single installed layer, replacing:

  • Framing
  • Insulation
  • Weather wrap
  • Drywall / sheathing
  • Exterior facade
  • Finishing materials

The result is a tighter building that substantially reduces HVAC load and electricity consumption compared to built-up construction.

Profiles

Four face
profiles.

Four standard face profiles to match the look of any project.

Flat

A smooth, unribbed face for the most minimal, monolithic appearance.

Box

Flat pans separated by narrow ribs, the cleanest, most contemporary look.

Trapezoidal

Repeating angular ribs for a bold, structured profile with added rigidity, common on exterior wall and roof panels.

Embossed

A subtle textured stucco finish that hides minor surface imperfections and reduces the appearance of oil-canning.

External face profile

Wave. Continuous rounded corrugations for added stiffness and a classic industrial profile.

Our engineering data lists three external face profiles: Box, Wave and Flat. Box and Flat are shown above.
